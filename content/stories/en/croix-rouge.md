# French Red Cross: a training platform for first-aiders

A volunteer contribution to a collective first-aid training platform.

## Starting with the need

The IA & Crise hackathon, organised by the French Red Cross and Fondation Simplon on 4–5 April 2025, brought the team together around a training problem. The collective project, crAIsis GENerator, combines revision, quizzes and assistance for first-aid training.

## My contribution

At the hackathon, I built the front end and features of a chatbot for first-aiders, and co-built its RAG over a 700-page manual with a third-party platform’s tools.

Over the following six months, as a volunteer with the team, I co-developed the app, front and back end, to help first-aiders keep their training up to date: more realistic role-play scenarios, worked out with domain experts, flashcards, and help on the Socratic coach, which puts first-aiders in a situation and asks them to explain how they would act. The platform’s advanced document retrieval was built by other team members.

## Conversations at ChangeNOW

At ChangeNOW 2025, I stayed with the team at the stand to discuss the project with the French Red Cross and visitors, and listen to their questions. A colleague delivered the English pitch on a small stage.

The project finished 1st of 12 teams at the IA & Crise hackathon, and the platform was tested with about 50 first-aiders. In July 2025, Pierre Ly, from the French Red Cross innovation team, presented it at the AI for Good summit in Geneva on the team’s behalf ([his post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7348294640778797059/)).

The experience combines two things I enjoy: contributing to a tool and discussing it directly with the people concerned.

## Result and limitations

The team platform was presented publicly. The code written after the hackathon stayed inside the Red Cross: my public fork shows the hackathon software, not that code. No measured educational outcome is claimed.
